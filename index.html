Everything the front desk and the chair need

One system for the whole practice

Nine connected modules that share one patient record — so the work you do in one place shows up everywhere it matters.

Patient Records

A complete chart per patient — demographics, medical history questionnaire, photos, and a full clinical timeline in one place.

Appointments & Booking

A month calendar, patient self-booking with daily slot limits per visit reason, and a request queue you approve and link to patients.

Dental Charting

An anatomical odontogram on the FDI quadrant cross — per-tooth conditions rendered as silhouettes, filled by status at a glance.

Progress Notes & Billing

Rich clinical notes that double as the billing form — fees, discounts, commissions, and next-visit scheduling flow straight into finance.

Orthodontics

Contract-based ortho with a payment passbook — deposits, per-visit installments, adjustments, and running balances tracked automatically.

Finance & Reports

Income and expense ledgers with an executive query bar, PDF exports, dentist-commission statements, and charted reporting.

Lab Cases

Track work sent to dental labs — case type, shade, due dates, job orders, and fees — so nothing gets lost between appointments.

Inventory

Keep supplies stocked with quantity tracking and reorder alerts, so the clinic never runs short mid-procedure.

Role-based Access

Owner, admin, dentist, and staff roles plus a granular per-user permission matrix — everyone sees exactly what they should.

How it works

From booking to billing, without the busywork

The modules talk to each other. A progress note bills itself, sets the next appointment, and updates finance — so your team charts once and the paperwork follows.

  • 1Book online or at the desk — daily slot limits keep the schedule sane.
  • 2Chart the visit and write a progress note that bills itself.
  • 3Finance, commissions, and next appointment update automatically.
  • 4Export ledgers and commission statements as ready-to-file PDFs.
